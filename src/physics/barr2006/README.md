# Barr et al. (2006) uncertainty curves, digitised

Curves of the atmospheric-flux uncertainties of G. D. Barr, T. K. Gaisser, S. Robbins and T. Stanev,
*Uncertainties in atmospheric neutrino fluxes*, Phys. Rev. D 74, 094009 (2006), digitised as (x, y) point lists:
up/down and up/horizontal ratios for νe and νμ, and the ν/ν̄ and νμ/νe ratios (x: neutrino energy in GeV,
y: uncertainty in %). The source figure panels were not recorded.

They are **not loaded by any code**. They are kept as the provenance of the closed-form uncertainty
parameterisations in `atm_flux.jl` (`Barr` systematics model), which were fitted to curves of this kind.

Index: https://newtrinos-org.github.io/newtrinos-index/records/barr-2006-atm-flux-uncertainties/
