Experiments

Newtrinos.jl includes likelihoods for more than 30 neutrino data sets across atmospheric, accelerator, reactor, solar, CEvNS and neutrino-mass experiments. Every experiment exposes the same interface — configure(), forward_model, params, priors, assets, and plot — so analysis code written for one experiment transfers directly to any other. See Configuring Experiments for a hands-on walkthrough.

Good starting points
  • Daya Bay (Newtrinos.dayabay) for reactor neutrinos — the compact module has no detector systematics, so the only free parameters are the oscillation parameters. Great for a first likelihood evaluation.
  • IceCube DeepCore (Newtrinos.deepcore) for atmospheric neutrinos — the most complete example of the full physics stack (oscillations, flux, Earth density, cross-sections).

Each experiment defines its own default_physics(); pass a NamedTuple of configured physics modules to configure to use a different model (e.g. a BSM oscillation model or a cross-section model shared between experiments).

Atmospheric Neutrinos

Atmospheric neutrino experiments detect neutrinos produced by cosmic-ray interactions in the upper atmosphere. Because the neutrinos travel through varying amounts of the Earth depending on their arrival direction, these experiments are simultaneously sensitive to $\theta_{23}$, $\Delta m^2_{31}$, and Earth-matter effects. They require the full physics stack: oscillations with matter effects (SI), the HKKM atmospheric flux, the PREM Earth density model, and a neutrino cross-section model.

Required physics modules: osc (with interaction = SI()), atm_flux, earth_layers, xsec.

Accelerator Neutrinos

Accelerator-based experiments use a $\nu_\mu$ ($\bar\nu_\mu$) beam produced from pion decays at a proton target. The known beam composition and energy spectrum allow precise measurements of $\theta_{23}$, $\Delta m^2_{31}$, $\delta_{CP}$ and sterile-neutrino mixing.

ExperimentModuleDataset
T2KNewtrinos.t2k2025 data release (FHC/RHC μ-like, e-like and νμ CC1π⁺ samples)
NOvANewtrinos.nova2024 data release, 10 years (νμ quartiles, νe core/peripheral/low-energy)
MINOSNewtrinos.minosSterile neutrino search, $16 \times 10^{20}$ POT

T2K and NOvA reweight the released predictions with oscillation probabilities averaged over an effective, calibrated energy response. All reference-point quantities are computed with the module's default (standard 3ν) physics, so the same released predictions are used whatever oscillation model is configured.

Required physics modules: osc (with interaction = SI() for T2K and NOvA; sterile models supported for MINOS), xsec.

Reactor Neutrinos

Reactor experiments detect electron antineutrinos ($\bar{\nu}_e$) produced by $\beta$-decay of fission products in nuclear power plants, via inverse beta decay. They provide the cleanest measurements of $\theta_{13}$ and $\Delta m^2_{31}$ (km baselines) and of $\Delta m^2_{21}$ (KamLAND, ~180 km).

ExperimentModuleDataset
Daya BayNewtrinos.dayabay3158 days, published spectra, near/far comparison (compact)
Daya BayNewtrinos.dayabay_rewriteFull data release: 8 ADs × 3 periods, full reactor and detector model
KamLANDNewtrinos.kamland2002–2012, three data-taking periods (dataset = :kamland2011 for the 2002–2009 data)
JUNONewtrinos.junoSimulated (design sensitivity)
TAONewtrinos.taoSimulated (design sensitivity)
  • dayabay_rewrite follows the official reproduction of the Daya Bay analysis: reactor flux model (dayabay_flux: Huber–Mueller spectra, non-equilibrium and spent-fuel corrections, free spectrum correction), IBD cross section (ibd_xsec, Vogel–Beacom), IAV, non-linearity and resolution, backgrounds, and per-detector and per-reactor nuisance parameters (181 parameters). The data release ships with the package.
  • kamland computes the reactor prediction from first principles with reactor_flux (IAEA PRIS power histories of all Japanese and Korean reactors, Huber–Mueller spectra anchored to Bugey-4) and uses matter effects (SI).
Note

JUNO and TAO are simulated experiments with no observed data. Their forward_model generates expected event spectra against an Asimov dataset, making them useful for sensitivity studies and future projections.

Required physics modules: osc (Vacuum() for Daya Bay, JUNO, TAO; SI() for KamLAND), plus reactor_flux (KamLAND) or dayabay_flux and ibd_xsec (dayabay_rewrite).

Solar Neutrinos

Solar neutrino experiments measure the νe flux from the Sun, converted by the MSW effect in the Sun and partially regenerated in the Earth at night. They constrain $\theta_{12}$ and $\Delta m^2_{21}$ and the solar fluxes. The solar modules share the standard solar model (solar_flux, B23), the ν–e scattering and capture cross sections (solar_xsec), and the solar oscillation probabilities (osc.solar_prob, adiabatic or with non-adiabatic level crossing, with Earth regeneration through the PREM zones).

Use either the SK day/night spectra (default) or configure(physics; daynight = :combined) together with sk_solar_dn, which carries the day/night information as in SK's own analysis.

Required physics modules: osc (SI()), solar_flux, solar_xsec, earth_layers.

Coherent Elastic Neutrino-Nucleus Scattering (CEvNS)

The COHERENT experiments at the Spallation Neutron Source (SNS) at Oak Ridge measure Coherent Elastic neutrino-Nucleus Scattering. They include their own SNS flux model (sns_flux) and CEvNS cross-section model (cevns_xsec), and do not depend on any oscillation physics. They are primarily used to constrain the weak mixing angle, nuclear form factors, and BSM couplings such as NSI.

ExperimentModuleDataset
COHERENT CsINewtrinos.coherent_csiCsI[Na] full-dataset release (arXiv:2110.07730; PRL 129, 081801)
COHERENT LArNewtrinos.coherent_lArCENNS-10 liquid argon, Analysis A release (doi:10.5281/zenodo.3903810)

Required physics modules: none (self-contained).

These modules translate the oscillation model's mass spectrum into the measured observables, e.g. for models with towers of neutrino states.

Validation

All implementations are validated against the publications: the Newtrinos verifications site shows, for each paper, the published figures next to the Newtrinos reproduction, with the scripts, pinned Newtrinos commits and caveats.

In addition, the subdirectories under src/experiments/ contain test.jl scripts that run the forward model and reproduce plots from the corresponding published analysis:

# Run from the Newtrinos.jl root directory
cd src/experiments/icecube/deepcore_8y_verification_sample
julia --project=../../../.. test.jl
