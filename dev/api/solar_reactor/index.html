Solar and Reactor Physics API

Solar Flux

The standard solar model is accessed via Newtrinos.solar_flux (B23, default MB22-met composition): fluxes and spectra of the pp, pep, hep, ⁷Be, ⁸B, ¹³N, ¹⁵O and ¹⁷F neutrinos, their production regions in the Sun (input to osc.solar_prob), and the flux normalisations with the correlated B23 prior (CorrelatedSSMPriors, default, as one vector parameter solar_norms) or as independent scalars (SSMPriors). The ⁸B spectrum is Winter et al. (2006) by default (b8_spectrum = :bahcall for Bahcall et al. 1996).

Newtrinos.solar_flux.SolarFluxConfig — Type
SolarFluxConfig{M<:SolarModel, S<:SolarFluxSystematics}

Fields

  • model::M = B23(): standard solar model.
  • systematics::S = CorrelatedSSMPriors(): flux uncertainty treatment.
  • n_production::Int = 40: number of radial sample points per component used to average the oscillation probability over the production region.
  • b8_spectrum::Symbol = :winter: nominal ⁸B spectrum, :winter (Winter et al. 2006, the spectrum used by SK, SNO and Borexino) or :bahcall (Bahcall et al. 1996); see read_spectra.
source
Newtrinos.solar_flux.SolarFlux — Type
SolarFlux <: Newtrinos.Physics

Configured solar neutrino flux model.

Fields

  • cfg::SolarFluxConfig: configuration.
  • params, priors: flux nuisance parameters and their priors.
  • nominal::NamedTuple: total flux per component [cm⁻² s⁻¹].
  • fractional_error::NamedTuple: solar-model fractional uncertainty per component.
  • correlation::Matrix{Float64}: solar-model correlation matrix, in COMPONENTS order.
  • production::NamedTuple: per component, the production region (r, ne, nn, w, profile) with r in R☉, electron and neutron densities in mol/cm³, weights summing to 1, and the solar density profile profile = (r, ne, nn) (used for non-adiabatic conversion); the format expected by Newtrinos.osc solar_prob.
  • flux::Function: flux(comp, params), total flux of a component [cm⁻² s⁻¹].
  • spectrum::Function: spectrum(comp, E, params), differential flux dΦ/dE [cm⁻² s⁻¹ MeV⁻¹] of a continuous component at energies E [MeV].
  • lines::NamedTuple: energies [MeV] and branching ratios of the mono-energetic components.
source
Newtrinos.solar_flux.B23 — Type
B23 <: SolarModel

B23 standard solar models (Herrera & Serenelli 2023, v1.2, doi:10.5281/zenodo.10822316; concept doi:10.5281/zenodo.10174170), computed with SF-III nuclear rates. This is the solar model used in NuFIT 6.0. Fluxes, uncertainties and correlations are the package's fluxes_SF3_*.dat; the structure tables (b23_*_structure.csv, centre to surface) are written from its struct+nu_SF3_*.dat by solar/make_b23_structure.py.

Fields

  • composition::Symbol = :MB22m: solar surface composition, one of :MB22m (Magg et al. 2022, meteoritic; NuFIT 6.0 reference), :MB22p, :AAG21, :GS98, :AGSS09, :C11.
source
Newtrinos.solar_flux.CorrelatedSSMPriors — Type
CorrelatedSSMPriors <: SolarFluxSystematics

Flux normalisations as one vector parameter solar_norms (nominal 1, entries in COMPONENTS order) with the correlated solar-model prior MvNormal(1, Σ), where Σ is built from the fractional uncertainties and the correlation matrix of the solar model (see ssm_prior), plus solar_b8_shape as in SSMPriors. Default.

source
Newtrinos.solar_flux.SSMPriors — Type
SSMPriors <: SolarFluxSystematics

One normalisation parameter per flux component (solar_norm_pp, ..., nominal 1) with a Gaussian prior of the solar-model fractional uncertainty, truncated to positive values, plus solar_b8_shape (prior Normal(0, 1)) shifting the ⁸B spectral shape by its ±1σ variation (positive values give a harder spectrum).

The priors are independent, so single components can be fixed, scanned or freed by name; CorrelatedSSMPriors includes the solar-model correlations.

source
Newtrinos.solar_flux.configure — Function
configure(cfg::SolarFluxConfig=SolarFluxConfig()) -> SolarFlux

Create a configured solar flux physics module.

Examples

sf = Newtrinos.solar_flux.configure()
sf.spectrum(:b8, [5.0, 10.0], sf.params)          # dΦ/dE [cm⁻² s⁻¹ MeV⁻¹]
osc.solar_prob(E_GeV, sf.production.b8, osc_params) # oscillation probability

Newtrinos.solar_flux.nadir_exposure — Function
nadir_exposure(latitude_deg; cz_edges=range(-1, 1, length=41), n_days=730, n_hours=720)
    -> NamedTuple

Yearly distribution of the Sun's direction at a detector at geographic latitude latitude_deg, weighted by the solar neutrino flux (∝ 1/d², with d the Earth–Sun distance).

Returns (cz_edges, cz, w) where w[k] is the fraction of the yearly flux arriving with cos(zenith) in bin k; cz is the cosine of the zenith angle of the Sun, i.e. of the direction the neutrinos come from, so cz < 0 is night (Earth crossing), in the convention of Newtrinos.earth_layers.compute_paths.

source
Newtrinos.solar_flux.read_spectra — Function
read_spectra(b8_spectrum=:winter) -> NamedTuple

Normalised spectral shapes [MeV⁻¹] of the continuous components as interpolators. For ⁸B, the best estimate and the shapes at ±3σ of solar_b8_shape, (best, soft3, hard3).

The ±3σ variations are those of Bahcall et al. (1996), applied as differences to the nominal spectrum. With b8_spectrum = :winter the nominal is Winter et al., PRC 73, 025503 (2006), Table IV (recommended by Solar fusion II), which is 1.1σ harder than Bahcall's best estimate. Its own quoted 1σ is only a quarter of Bahcall's, but the later α-spectrum measurements differ from it by up to ≈5 % below 15 MeV (Solar fusion III, Sec. II.D.3), so the wider Bahcall variation is kept. The table ends at 15.2 MeV; above, Bahcall's shape is scaled to match (≈10⁻⁵ of the flux).

Solar Cross-Sections

ν–e elastic scattering (with radiative corrections) and the ³⁷Cl and ⁷¹Ga capture cross sections, accessed via Newtrinos.solar_xsec.

Newtrinos.solar_xsec.SolarXsec — Type
SolarXsec <: Newtrinos.Physics

Detection cross sections for solar neutrino experiments.

Fields

  • params, priors: cl_xsec_sigma and ga_xsec_sigma, cross-section uncertainties of the radiochemical experiments in units of standard deviations (prior Normal(0, 1)).
  • dσdT_es::Function: dσdT_es(flavour, E, T), differential ν–e elastic scattering cross section [cm² MeV⁻¹] for neutrino energy E and electron recoil kinetic energy T [MeV]; flavour is :e or :x (νμ or ντ).
  • capture::Function: capture(target, E, params), absorption cross section [cm²] on target :cl37 or :ga71 at neutrino energy E [MeV].

Reactor Flux

Reactor ν̄e fluxes and IBD detection shared by reactor experiments (Newtrinos.reactor_flux): Huber–Mueller isotope spectra, energy per fission, IBD cross section and kinematics, Bugey-4 rate anchoring, and a reactor database (src/physics/reactors/: IAEA PRIS units of Japan and Korea with thermal power, load factors and site coordinates).

Newtrinos.reactor_flux.ReactorFlux — Type
ReactorFlux <: Newtrinos.Physics

Fields

  • isotope_spectrum(iso, E): ν̄e per fission per MeV of isotope iso ∈ ISOTOPES at energy E [MeV].
  • spectrum(E, fractions): ν̄e per fission per MeV for fission fractions fractions (NamedTuple over ISOTOPES).
  • ibd_xsec(E): IBD cross section [cm²].
  • fission_rate(P_MW, fractions): fissions per second at thermal power P_MW [MW].
source
Newtrinos.reactor_flux.bugey4_scale — Function
bugey4_scale(fractions) -> Float64

Rate normalisation anchored to the Bugey-4 measurement (Declais et al. 1994: ⟨σ_f⟩ = 5.752×10⁻⁴³ cm²/fission at fission fractions 0.538 : 0.078 : 0.328 : 0.056), corrected for the fuel difference with the Huber–Mueller isotope yields, relative to the plain Huber–Mueller yield (KamLAND, PRD 88, 033001, Eq. 1).

source
Newtrinos.reactor_flux.neutrino_energy — Function
neutrino_energy(E_prompt) -> Float64

Antineutrino energy [MeV] whose mean prompt energy positron_energy(E) + M_E is E_prompt [MeV] (inverse of positron_energy; E − E_prompt grows from 0.784 MeV at threshold to 0.84 MeV at E = 8 MeV).

source
Newtrinos.reactor_flux.positron_energy — Function
positron_energy(E) -> Float64

Mean total positron energy [MeV] of IBD at antineutrino energy E [MeV], including the neutron recoil (Strumia & Vissani (2003), Eq. (16): ⟨Ee⟩ ≈ (E₁ + E₂)/2 = E − δ − E^CMν E^CMe / mp, better than 1 % below 100 MeV). The prompt (visible) energy of the event is positron_energy(E) + M_E.

source
Newtrinos.reactor_flux.load_units — Function
load_units(; countries) -> DataFrame

Power reactor units (PRIS) with thermal power, site coordinates and annual operating history (hours online, load factor).

source
Newtrinos.reactor_flux.daily_power — Function
daily_power(unit, from::Date, to::Date) -> Vector{Float64}

Daily mean thermal power [MW] of a unit, from its annual load factor (PRIS): within a year the operating time is placed contiguously before a suspension/shutdown date in that year, at the end of the year after a year without operation (restart), at the start of the year before a year without operation, and uniformly otherwise.

IBD Cross-Section and Daya Bay Flux

Newtrinos.ibd_xsec provides inverse-beta-decay cross sections (Strumia–Vissani table, Vogel–Beacom first order); Newtrinos.dayabay_flux the reactor flux model of the full Daya Bay analysis (Huber–Mueller spectra, non-equilibrium and spent-fuel corrections, free spectrum correction), used by Newtrinos.dayabay_rewrite.

Newtrinos.ibd_xsec.StrumiaVissani — Type
StrumiaVissani <: IBDModel

Tabulated cross-section of Strumia and Vissani (https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0302055).

source
Newtrinos.ibd_xsec.VogelBeacomO1 — Type
VogelBeacomO1 <: IBDModel

Differential IBD cross-section dσ/dcosθ to first order in 1/M (Vogel & Beacom, https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9903554), with the exact kinematic relation Eν(Ee, cosθ) and the Jacobian dEν/dEe, as used in the official Daya Bay full-data analysis (dgmreactorneutrino). Here the functions take the deposited energy Edep = Ee + mₑ: xsec(Enu, cosθ) [1e-41 cm²], Enu(Edep, cosθ) [MeV], jacobian(Enu, Edep, cosθ) (dEν/dEdep). Constants: PDG 2024 masses and neutron lifetime, f = 1, g = 1.2701, f₂ = 3.706, phase space factor 1.71465.

source
Newtrinos.dayabay_flux.get_flux — Function
get_flux(cfg; datadir)

Returns reactor_flux(E, thermal_power_scale, energy_per_fission, fission_fractions_scale, pulls, neq_scale, snf_scale, reac_idx) -> (main, neq, snf, correction) at antineutrino energies E (number or vector): the antineutrino spectrum per GW thermal power [ν/s/MeV/GW] and the extra flux from the non-equilibrium correction, the SNF flux [ν/s/MeV] of a reactor at nominal power, and the free spectrum correction, which multiplies only main (as in the official model). For repeated evaluation on a fixed mesh use get_flux_on_mesh.

source
Newtrinos.dayabay_flux.get_flux_on_mesh — Function
get_flux_on_mesh(cfg; datadir)

Returns E -> flux where flux(thermal_power_scale, energy_per_fission, fission_fractions_scale, pulls, neq_scale, snf_scale, reac_idx) gives (main, neq, snf, correction) at the antineutrino energies E (see get_flux). Everything that does not depend on the parameters (isotope spectra, NEQ and SNF corrections, interpolation weights of the free spectrum correction) is tabulated once for the mesh E.

Published Δχ² maps

Newtrinos.chi2map turns a published Δχ² table into an experiment (or a penalty term) that can be added to a joint likelihood, for data sets whose full likelihood is not available.

Newtrinos.chi2map.Chi2Map — Type

A tabulated χ² as a likelihood term: χ²(x₁, …, xn) on a regular grid, where each coordinate xk is a function of the parameters (e.g. p -> sin(p.θ₁₂)^2), interpolated with cubic splines (ForwardDiff-safe, linear extrapolation outside the grid). Used to add the profiled likelihood of a whole sector (e.g. the solar data, or published χ² maps such as Super-Kamiokande's atmospheric one) to a fit:

configure(coordinates, axes, chi2; title="χ² map") -> Chi2Map
  • coordinates: tuple of functions params -> x_k;
  • axes: tuple of AbstractRanges (uniform grid along each coordinate);
  • chi2: array of size length.(axes) (an offset is irrelevant for profiles and is removed).

The term contributes −χ²/2 to the log-likelihood and has no parameters of its own.

source
Newtrinos.chi2map.Chi2Penalty — Type
Chi2Penalty(χ²)

Pseudo-distribution whose log-density is −χ²/2 for any observation (the χ² term of a Chi2Map).
